How a scene gets made
Step 01
Pick from the table, not from a blank file.
Fifty-five scenes, each with a length somebody measured. Reading the row takes ten seconds; inventing the layout takes an afternoon.
Step 02
Take it with video new, never by hand.
That is what points the copy at the local GSAP and fills the engine slots. A file dragged out by hand keeps a CDN address and needs the network at render time.
Step 03
Replace every line of the sample copy.
All of it is real writing, not placeholders, so nothing flags what you missed. Keep roughly the length it was drawn for — a headline three times longer overflows silently.
Step 04
Render it, then read the render back.
A failed font fetch is not an error, it is a scene that came out wrong with nothing in the output saying so. Check the frames, do not assume them.
